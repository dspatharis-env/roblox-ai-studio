# Roblox Business Plan

## Goal
Create and improve Roblox experiences that can generate sustainable Roblox-native revenue.

## Growth model
Acquisition -> onboarding -> retention -> engagement -> monetization -> reinvestment.

## Revenue principles
- Roblox-native monetization only.
- Prioritize fun, retention, and replayability before aggressive monetization.
- Instrument purchase funnels and economy health.
- Avoid pay-to-win mechanics that damage long-term retention unless explicitly tested and justified.
- Human owner approves pricing, spending, publishing, and financial decisions.
