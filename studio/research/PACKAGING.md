# Roblox Packaging Research — 2026-09-26

## Official findings
Sources:
- https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery
- https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails
- https://create.roblox.com/docs/get-started/strategies

### What matters
- Roblox says recommendation quality is influenced by post-click behavior including play-through, bounce, repeat play, playtime and monetization.
- Packaging (icon, thumbnails, media and description) can improve play-through and retention, but it must accurately represent the game.
- Roblox explicitly recommends unique metadata and original imagery rather than repetitive/copycat naming and visuals.
- Home-page thumbnail personalization activates with 2+ thumbnails; Roblox recommends 2–5 active thumbnail variants.
- Image thumbnails should be 16:9, ideally 1920x1080, with important content kept away from the bottom metadata area.

## Studio packaging rule
For each active game, prepare:
1. one strong square icon concept
2. three 16:9 gameplay-accurate thumbnail concepts
3. one concise description hook
4. one visual system (palette, type, recurring motif)
5. after traffic exists, test multiple thumbnail variants instead of assuming a winner

Never use AI imagery that promises mechanics or visual fidelity that the actual Roblox experience does not deliver.
