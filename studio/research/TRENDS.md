# Trends — Working Interpretation

## Durable rather than cosmetic trends
- short, instantly understandable verbs
- highly readable progression
- social tension/cooperation
- collection and rarity
- event-driven return reasons
- strong mobile-first UI
- shareable visual moments
- frequent but meaningful content updates

## Studio response
### Boop Arena
Compete on clarity, escalating arena pressure and playful social moments.

### Steal a Critter
Compete on readable ownership/stealing drama, collection and base expression.

### Sprout Market
Compete on mutation discovery + social market identity.

### Speed Escape
Compete on speed mastery + spectacle + route decisions.

## Anti-trend rule
Do not rename/reskin a top game and reproduce its exact loop. Roblox itself warns that non-unique games/metadata may perform poorly in ads and discovery.
