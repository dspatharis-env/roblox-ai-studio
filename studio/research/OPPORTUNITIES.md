# Opportunity Framework

New opportunities are scored qualitatively, never by copying the current top chart.

For each idea record:
- player fantasy
- one-sentence core loop
- why now
- comparable experiences
- what we deliberately do differently
- 30-second first-fun plan
- 10-minute session plan
- social hook
- retention hook
- monetization fit
- technical risk
- art/content cost
- mobile risk
- policy risk
- smallest prototype
- kill criteria

## Portfolio rule
Do not start a fifth game while Boop Arena still lacks validated retention data unless the new idea is clearly cheaper to validate and the owner explicitly changes priority.
