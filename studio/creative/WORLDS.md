# Boop Arena Worlds

Only Boop Arena world concepts are active.

## Design hierarchy
Gameplay silhouette → collapse behavior → spawn fairness → sightlines → theme → decoration.

## Arena 1: Classic Square
Purpose: baseline, open movement, easiest for new players to read.

## Arena 2: Bridge Cross
Purpose: chokepoints, four directional escape routes, risky center control.

## Arena 3: Broken Donut
Purpose: perimeter movement, center gap pressure, changing escape paths.

## Theme candidates
Themes are skins on validated layouts:
- toy-cloud
- candy-sky
- neon-rooftop
- floating-garden
- toy-castle

## Higgsfield output checklist
A useful concept must make it easy to identify:
- playable floor
- danger/falling tiles
- void/edge
- player silhouettes
- safe lobby or spectator reference if visible

No concept should require dense high-poly scenery to look good.
