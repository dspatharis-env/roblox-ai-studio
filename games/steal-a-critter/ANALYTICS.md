# Steal a Critter Analytics Spec

## Core questions
- Can a new player buy their first critter quickly?
- Do players understand passive income?
- Does first steal feel exciting or frustrating?
- Are locks strategic or merely annoying?
- Does the economy inflate too fast after rebirth?

## First-session funnel
1. joined
2. base_assigned
3. conveyor_viewed
4. first_critter_bought
5. first_critter_placed
6. first_income_tick_seen
7. first_lock_used
8. first_steal_attempt
9. first_successful_steal

## Economy sources
- passive critter income
- other rewards
## Sinks
- critter purchase
- rebirth
- future upgrades

## Critical segmentation
- new vs returning
- rebirth tier
- payer vs non-payer
- victim vs thief session
- rarity tier

## Watch-outs
If stealing improves session time but materially reduces D1/D7 retention, the friction is too high.
