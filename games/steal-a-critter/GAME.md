# Steal a Critter — v0.1.10

## Pitch
Buy meme critters from a conveyor, place them in your base to earn cash every second, and try to steal rare critters from rival bases while protecting your own.

## Current systems
- Code-built island, conveyor and 8 player bases.
- 10 critters across five rarities: Common 60%, Uncommon 25%, Rare 10%, Epic 4%, Legendary 1%.
- Passive income per critter with persistent saves.
- Hold-to-steal loop with 45 s carry window, owner reclaim and anti-teleport validation.
- Base lock: 60 s active, 60 s recharge.
- Rebirth: starts at $10,000, cost x3 each rebirth, max 10, +50% income each.
- Spooky Season event: 16 Oct–3 Nov, three limited critters and countdown.
- Funnel/economy analytics.
- Session-safe save protection and failed-load protection.
- 9 feature switches.
