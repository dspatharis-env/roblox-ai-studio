# Sprout Market — Design

## Pitch
Grow crops in real time, harvest them, discover rare mutated plants and sell or trade them with friends.

## Planned loop
1. Claim a garden plot and buy seeds.
2. Plant crops that keep growing while offline.
3. Harvest and sell for currency.
4. Seed shop restocks every 5 minutes.
5. Weather can trigger mutations.
6. Safe two-party trading requires confirmation from both players.

## Monetization concepts
- Faster Growth pass
- Extra Plot pass
- Seed Restock developer product

## Differentiation direction
Make mutations visually collectible and socially desirable, with readable weather-driven events and a warm market-town identity instead of copying another garden game's exact presentation.
