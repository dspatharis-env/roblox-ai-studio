# Sprout Market Product Strategy

## Core fantasy
"I planted something ordinary and came back to discover something rare and beautiful."

## Differentiation
Do not compete only on crop count. Build identity around:
- mutation discovery
- weather anticipation
- a social market square
- visible garden personality
- safe two-sided trading

## First prototype proof
Need to prove:
1. planting feels satisfying
2. offline growth is understandable
3. harvest gives a visible payoff
4. shop restock creates anticipation without frustration
5. one mutation event creates a memorable moment

## Policy/economy note
If seed outcomes ever become random and purchasable with Robux-linked currency, paid-random-item rules may apply. Prefer deterministic seed purchasing in early versions.
