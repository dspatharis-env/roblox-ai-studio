# Speed Escape — Design

## Pitch
Every step makes you faster. Race up a checkpoint track while a rising wave chases the lobby, buy upgrades and rebirth for stronger multipliers.

## Planned loop
1. Enter the track.
2. Gain speed while moving.
3. Reach checkpoints before the wave.
4. Earn coins and upgrade.
5. Rebirth for a multiplier.
6. Replay at higher speed and risk.

## Monetization concepts
- 2x Speed pass
- Auto-Run pass
- Rebirth Skip product

## Differentiation direction
Focus on spectacular readable wave events, route choices, escalating speed mastery and short restart times rather than a generic straight-line simulator.
