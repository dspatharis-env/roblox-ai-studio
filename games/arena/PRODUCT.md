# Boop Arena Product Strategy

## Positioning
A friendly physics party knockout game where the arena itself becomes the timer.

## Why the loop can work
- instant verb: Boop
- readable social target: nearby player
- escalating arena pressure
- short rounds create natural replay
- eliminations create spectate/anticipation moments
- cosmetics fit the playful identity without affecting fairness

## Biggest current risks
1. 12-second lobby wait may feel long to a first-time user if nothing interactive happens.
2. New players may not understand facing/range of Boop.
3. 90-second cap may feel slow with low player counts.
4. 11x11 single shape can become visually repetitive.
5. elimination can create dead time if spectating/requeue is weak.
6. Double Coins alone is not a compelling long-term monetization strategy.

## High-value improvements after ar-03
- new-player "try a boop" micro-tutorial in lobby
- immediate spectate + quick next-round messaging
- visible danger telegraph beyond only red color
- round-start countdown under 3 seconds after teleport
- post-round instant requeue feedback
- cosmetics tied to KOs/wins/seasonal play
- party/friend invite surface after a good round

## ar-03 design rule
The three arena shapes must change movement decisions, not just silhouette.
Candidate archetypes:
- Classic Square: baseline
- Cross / bridges: chokepoints and recovery routes
- Donut / broken ring: center hazard and perimeter fights

Each shape needs spawn fairness and clear collapse behavior.
