# Boop Arena Monetization Plan

## Phase 0 — before playtest
No paid prompt.

## Phase 1 — fair permanent pass
**Double Coins**
- only affects earn rate, not boop strength/range/cooldown
- show in explicit Shop
- do not auto-prompt on spawn
- ID remains 0 until owner creates pass

## Phase 2 — cosmetics
Coins become useful for:
- boop impact VFX skins
- trails
- victory poses
- nameplate accents
- lobby emotes
No gameplay advantage.

## Phase 3 — optional repeatable spend
Only after healthy retention:
- deterministic cosmetic bundles or currency packs
Avoid paid random crates by default.

## Metrics
- shop_open_rate
- pass_view_rate
- purchase_prompt_rate
- purchase_completion_rate
- payer conversion
- D1 retention segmented by shop exposure
